# /schools/alpha-girls — surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a Dar es Salaam parent choosing a girls' secondary
for Form 1 or Form 5, on a mid-range Android phone, comparing schools on
results and trust. Job: read the school like a prospectus, in order, and
apply.

User's direction (2026-10-22, replacing the results-led showcase of
2026-10-21): the same organised order as Alpha High: who the school is, what
it teaches, how its students perform, life outside class, who runs it, how
to join. "Bold and youthful, not like a corporate site." Sea-green and gold
stay.

Content: unchanged from the previous build: NECTA's results for centre S5889
(CSEE 2024, CSEE 2025, ACSEE 2026), the 2020 opening and graduation
milestones, competitions, the enterprise pathway, clubs, the 2026 calendar,
the 17 subjects, option subjects, 12 combinations, entry criteria, every
unconfirmed note, the school-supplied photographs. Names of girls withheld.
