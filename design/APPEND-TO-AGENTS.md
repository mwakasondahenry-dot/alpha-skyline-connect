## Design references

Client-approved mockups live in `design/`.

**Read `design/README.md` before any visual work.** It indexes the mockups and
lists known factual errors in them — wrong company name, unverified statistics,
invented testimonial quotes — that must not be copied into the build.

`design/SESSION-BRIEFS.md` holds the working order and the exact brief for each
stage. Do not skip stages.

## Naming rule

- **ALPHA** is the school brand: logo, header wordmark, page titles, meta
  descriptions, marketing copy, social handles, domain, and the three school
  names (Alpha High, Alpha Girls, Alpha Nursery & Primary).
- **ALFA EDUCATION CENTRE** is the legal entity: footer copyright, postal
  address, "operated by" lines, terms and privacy pages, structured data legal
  name. Nowhere else.

The current code applies this correctly. Do not change it to match a mockup.

## Content placeholders

Text in square brackets, e.g. `[Fee structure per school — to be provided]`,
marks content the school still owes. Keep it visible and clearly marked. Never
invent replacement copy and never quietly delete the section.

## Git

Never force push, rebase, or amend a pushed commit. This branch syncs with
Lovable and rewriting history destroys the project history there.
